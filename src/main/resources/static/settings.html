Face Recognition Model

Face detection and face recognition each run on a datasource only when that datasource is configured for them. Detection needs no model. Recognition — grouping detected faces into people — requires an embedding model, chosen here once for the whole instance. Changing it re-groups every enabled datasource.

Your model must be a 112×112 ArcFace-aligned ONNX embedding model. It is fetched and tested on two known faces when you save; channel order and normalization are detected automatically. You are responsible for holding the rights to any model you point at.

How similar two faces must be to count as the same person. Lower merges more people together; higher splits one person across several. Saving re-groups every stored face — no images are re-processed, but group names are rebuilt and human names carried over where they still fit.
